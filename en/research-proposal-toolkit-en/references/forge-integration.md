# A complete workflow built on Forge

This package is built on [Research Question Forge](https://github.com/q1feng/research-question-forge). It is one complete implementation, not Forge's only use. A same-language core snapshot is included for standalone use; the host need not support Skill-to-Skill calls.

## Question formation

Enter at the current stage. Accept early ideas without a finished title. Load the embedded core as needed:

- [Question abstraction](forge/question-abstraction.md): turn observations, goals, and existing explanations into knowledge questions.
- [Literature feedback](forge/literature-feedback.md): nearest work, baselines, counterevidence, source checks, and handoffs.
- [Expansion and decomposition](forge/expansion-decomposition.md): broader significance, concrete footholds, scope, and revision costs.
- [Research judgments](forge/research-taste.md): discuss dimensions of value while preserving researcher choice.
- [Framework and revision](forge/framework-output.md): maintain one current record of positioning, evidence, and decisions.

## Stages and outputs

01 Question and difference checks → 02 Research design and evidence planning → 03 Review drafting → 04 Proposal drafting and coordinated revision. Stages express dependencies, not approval gates. Draft stable material while leaving unsettled central decisions as candidates. Respect a requested sequence such as review, further reading and revision, then proposal.

Gradually produce research-framework.md, citation-ledger.md, literature-review.md, and research-proposal.md; reuse equivalent existing files. Detailed task briefs are optional, not prerequisites for prose. Deliver only the requested stage when scope is limited; when full drafts are requested, do not stop at outlines.

## Replaceable capabilities

Use the researcher's preferred search, reading, citation-management, design, analysis, coding, writing, or layout capabilities without requiring a particular community suite. Supply the question, confirmed facts, materials, evidence requirements, and boundaries. Check returned sources and uncertainty; do not silently adopt a new research direction.

Suggest capability categories for the current task gap rather than requiring a full suite. When the user wants to discover external Skills, assess disciplinary fit, inputs and outputs, environment, and evidence requirements; explain the rationale and what remains unverified. Do not invent candidates without retrieval access. Installation and external actions require the applicable user authorization. Figure and presentation integrations have not been tested with this toolkit; do not claim out-of-the-box compatibility. Hand off only the materials needed and check returned claims, citations, and figure meanings for consistency. Presentation must not turn planned work into completed findings.

Without search, use supplied evidence and mark unchecked claims. Without layout tools, deliver text and do not claim verified pagination. Narrow unsupported claims or record gaps rather than fabricating completeness. Scripts, formal studies, and external actions remain within the user's actual authorization.
