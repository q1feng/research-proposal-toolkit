# Question expansion and decomposition

## Question Expansion: from a point to a broader problem

Identify a local tension or unexplained observation, examine potentially shared relations, mechanisms, or constraints, and consider a broader knowledge question and its significance. Specify what may vary, what must remain fixed, and what evidence generalization would require.

Expansion is not inflation. One event does not establish a general rule; a new application label is not automatically a contribution; a larger system does not necessarily support deeper research. Keep a local question when appropriate rather than requiring every study to discover a fundamental mechanism.

## Question Decomposition: from a broad problem to a foothold

When a question is important but too large, identify necessary sub-questions and select those that are consequential and answerable under actual conditions. Consider degree and research stage, time, data or materials, theoretical preparation, tools, evidence, and reproducibility. Do not invent resources or deadlines.

Build the chain: broad question → sub-question → bounded answerable question → evidence needed → feasible research foothold. The foothold should distinguish candidate explanations or approaches; changing a dataset or building a platform alone is insufficient. Allow broad significance while keeping scope manageable; do not mandate three contributions or work packages.

## Scope changes and decisions

When comparing contexts, check whether they share a mechanism or only a surface resemblance. Narrow claims, conditions, or objects when warranted. Explain why expansion, decomposition, or retaining scope is appropriate.

Use completed work, drafts, and the user's progress to offer meaningfully different small, moderate, or substantial revisions when needed. Explain value, reusable work, new evidence, rework, and uncertainty; not every situation needs all three options. The assistant advises and the researcher chooses the central direction. Implement an explicit choice without repeated confirmation. Do not change the topic merely to signal progress.
