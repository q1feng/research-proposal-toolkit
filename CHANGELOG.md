# Changelog

## v1.0

- Build on Research Question Forge for question formation and extend its framework into literature synthesis, citation auditing, research design, and coordinated review/proposal drafts.
- Connect new evidence and feedback to question revision, framework development, and writing, while preserving source traceability and the distinction between planned and completed work.
- Support institutional requirements and bilingual, standalone use, while encouraging users to compose their own search, revision, editing, and writing workflows. Figure and presentation integrations remain untested.

### 中文说明

- 基于 Research Question Forge 形成研究问题，将其研究框架继续发展为文献综合、引用审计、研究设计及联动的综述／开题草稿。
- 让新证据与反馈贯穿问题修订、框架调整和写作，保留来源追溯及拟开展工作与已完成成果的区分。
- 支持学校要求、中英文与免安装使用，鼓励按需组合检索、框架修订、润色和写作 Skill；作图与 PPT 扩展尚未经过集成测试。
